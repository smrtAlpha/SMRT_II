SMRT Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 21, 2026

SMRT is a study app that works online and offline. This page explains what information SMRT handles and why. We wrote it to be short and plain.

What SMRT keeps on your device

Your chats, the files you attach, your knowledge packs and your waiting research tasks are saved on your own device, in your browser's storage. We do not keep copies of them on our servers. If you clear your browser data, they are removed from that device.

Your account

You can use SMRT as a guest, or create an account with Google or with your email address. When you do, we receive your email address (and, from Google, your basic profile such as your name). We use it only to identify your account and to let you sign in again. Sign-in is handled by our provider, Supabase.

When you ask a question

To write an answer, SMRT sends the following to our server, which passes it to Google's Gemini service: your question, recent earlier messages from the same chat, the text of any file you attached to that chat, and the summary of a knowledge pack if you choose to use one. We do not store this content on our servers.

Google processes it under its own terms. Because SMRT currently uses Google's free Gemini service, Google may use submitted content to improve its products. Please do not send sensitive personal information such as passwords, ID numbers or bank details.

Research tasks

A research task you queue is sent to the same service when your device is back online. The result is saved on your device.

Usage limits

To keep SMRT fair and prevent abuse, we count how many requests each account makes in a period of time. These counts are stored with your account ID and contain no message content.

Voice typing

If you use the voice button, your browser records what you say and turns it into text using its own speech service (in Chrome, this is provided by Google). The audio is handled by your browser and that service, not stored by SMRT.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, SMRT shows them from your device when a research task finishes. No notification data is sent to us.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell your information.
  • We do not show ads.
  • We do not share your chats with other users.

Deleting your information

You can delete any chat, knowledge pack or research task inside SMRT, which removes it from your device. To delete your account and the information we hold about it, email us at the address below and we will do it.

Age

SMRT is meant for students aged 13 and older.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top of this page.
